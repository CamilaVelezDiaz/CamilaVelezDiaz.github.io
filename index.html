Taxonomic Assessment and Biodiversity Analysis of Historical Deep-Sea and Cross-Shelf Collections from Queensland Waters

Queensland museum collections contain thousands of deep-sea echinoderm specimens that remain unresolved to species level. This research integrates museum collections and biodiversity databases to identify taxonomic knowledge gaps across depth gradients in Northeast Australia and improve understanding of marine biodiversity patterns.
