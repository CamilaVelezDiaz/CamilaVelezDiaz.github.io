Taxonomic Assessment and Biodiversity Analysis of Historical Deep-Sea and Cross-Shelf Collections from Queensland Waters

Queensland museum collections contain thousands of deep-sea echinoderm specimens that remain unresolved to species level. This work integrates museum and biodiversity databases to identify taxonomic gaps and assess conservation priorities for these poorly understood deep-sea communities.
